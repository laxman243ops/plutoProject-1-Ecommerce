## Insight 1 — Beauty & Health is the Undisputed Revenue Leader
**Chart Reference:** Chart 1 — Revenue by Category

`beleza_saude` (Beauty & Health) generated **R$ 1,47,980.12** — the highest of all product categories, outperforming #2 `relogios_presentes` (R$ 1,32,362.31) by over 11%. The heatmap (Chart 6) confirms this category maintains consistent monthly sales across all 23 months of data — it is not a seasonal spike but a core, year-round revenue engine.

**Business Recommendation:**
Increase inventory depth and allocate 20–25% more marketing budget to beauty and health products year-round. Introduce a loyalty rewards program for repeat buyers in this category to reduce churn and increase customer lifetime value.

## Insight 2 — Revenue Grew 17× in 2 Years with Clear Seasonal Peaks
**Chart Reference:** Chart 2 — Monthly Sales Trend

Total monthly revenue grew from ~R$ 1,000 (Oct 2016) to ~R$ 17,000 (April 2018) — a **17× increase** in just 23 months. April 2018 was the single strongest month on record. Q2 (April–June) consistently outperforms all other quarters in both 2017 and 2018, indicating a seasonal demand cycle tied to mid-year consumer behaviour.

**Business Recommendation:**
Launch targeted promotional campaigns in February–March to pre-load Q2 demand and sustain the April peak. Ramp up logistics capacity and seller stock requirements before April each year to prevent supply-side shortfalls during the highest-traffic period.

## Insight 3 — São Paulo Dominance Creates a Regional Concentration Risk
**Chart Reference:** Chart 3 — Regional Sales

SP (São Paulo) generated **R$ 19,302.56** — nearly **3× more** than #2 RJ (R$ 7,080.74) and more than the combined revenue of states ranked 5th through 10th. This extreme geographic concentration means the business is disproportionately exposed to any economic or logistical disruption in São Paulo.

**Business Recommendation:**
Dedicate specific campaign budgets for underperforming states — CE, PB, SE, PR, RS, and BA — where market penetration is demonstrably low. Set a target of 10% regional revenue diversification within 12 months by offering state-specific promotions and faster delivery options in those regions.

## Insight 4 — 1-Star Reviews Are the Second Most Common Rating
**Chart Reference:** Chart 5 — Review Score Distribution

Despite a strong overall 5-star rating share of **56.4%**, the **1-star rating at 13.8%** is the second most frequent single score — surpassing both 2-star (3.9%) and 3-star (8.3%). This bimodal distribution — customers either love the experience or hate it — signals specific and addressable product or delivery failures rather than a general satisfaction problem.

**Business Recommendation:**
Cross-reference 1-star reviews with product category and delivery time data to identify which categories generate the most complaints. Implement minimum seller quality standards in high-revenue categories like `beleza_saude`. Target a 50% reduction in 1-star reviews within two quarters through direct seller performance monitoring.

---

## Insight 5 — Average Order Value is Declining and Most Orders Are Below R$ 300
**Chart Reference:** Chart 4 — Order Value Distribution

The order value histogram shows an extreme right-skew — the vast majority of orders fall below R$ 300, with a sharp peak under R$ 100. Average order value dropped from **R$ 253.30 (Oct 2016)** to **R$ 89.41 (Apr 2017)** — a 65% decline — before stabilising. High-value orders above R$ 1,000 are rare outliers.

**Business Recommendation:**
Introduce bundle offers and a minimum-spend incentive (e.g. "Spend R$ 300, get free delivery + 10% off") to reverse the AOV decline. Create curated product combinations in the top 3 categories to encourage larger basket sizes. Track AOV monthly as a leading indicator of revenue health alongside total order volume.

## 🔍 Most Surprising Finding

The most unexpected result was that `fashion_roupa_masculina` (men's clothing) and `papelaria` (stationery) show extreme dark-blue revenue spikes in only one or two months on the heatmap — then essentially disappear for the rest of the year. These appear to be viral or event-driven demand bursts rather than organic category growth. Despite these dramatic spikes, neither category appears in the top 10 by total annual revenue. The broader lesson is that consistent, steady-performing categories like `beleza_saude` generate far more cumulative value than one-time viral moments — making reliability more important than peak performance when planning inventory and marketing investment.
